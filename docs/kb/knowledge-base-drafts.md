## DOC: About Ethiopian Maids — who we are and what we do

Ethiopian Maids is a placement service that connects Ethiopian domestic
workers (housemaids, nannies, cooks, elderly-care helpers) with sponsor
families in the Gulf (GCC) countries: the UAE, Saudi Arabia, Kuwait, Qatar,
Bahrain, and Oman.

We work with Ethiopian candidates only, and we place them only in GCC
countries. We do not place workers of other nationalities and we do not
recruit for other roles (no drivers, private nurses, or company staff).

There are two sides to our service:
- Sponsors (families and employers) browse verified candidates, interview
  them by video call, and hire through us.
- Job seekers (Ethiopian women looking for domestic work) register in our
  app, build a profile, and apply to job openings posted by sponsors.

This WhatsApp number is our customer-service channel. Registration, profile
creation, browsing, and job applications all happen in the official
Ethiopian Maids app — never over chat. We never ask for passport numbers,
bank details, or payments in WhatsApp messages.

Our team speaks English, Arabic, and Amharic.

Ethiopian passport. Under Ethiopian overseas-employment law you will also

## DOC: UAE — rules for hiring a domestic worker

The UAE regulates domestic work under Federal Decree-Law No. 9 of 2022 (the
Domestic Workers Law), administered by MoHRE. Key points every sponsor
should know (current to early 2026 — our team re-checks these regularly):

- Hiring must go through licensed recruitment agencies / domestic-worker
  service centres, with a standard MoHRE contract.
- The worker must be at least 18 years old.
- Working conditions guaranteed by law: one paid rest day per week; at
  least 12 hours of rest per day (including 8 consecutive hours); 30 days
  of paid annual leave per year.
- The employer provides food, suitable accommodation, and medical
  insurance, and pays salaries on time (within 10 days of the due date).
- Recruitment costs are the employer's responsibility — it is illegal to
  charge them to the worker or deduct them from her salary.
- Keeping the worker's passport is prohibited; the passport belongs to the
  worker.
- A probation period applies at the start of the contract during which the
  agency is responsible for replacement under its terms.
- The employer sponsors the worker's residence visa, Emirates ID, and
  medical fitness testing.

There is no UAE statutory minimum wage for domestic workers, but source
countries set salary floors; typical market salaries for Ethiopian
housemaids are in the 1,200–1,500 AED/month range [confirm current market
range with our team].

## DOC: Saudi Arabia — rules for hiring a domestic worker

Saudi Arabia manages all domestic-worker recruitment through the government
Musaned platform, under the Domestic Workers Regulation updated in 2023
(current to early 2026 — our team re-checks these regularly):

- Contracts must be created and registered through Musaned; recruitment
  outside Musaned is not lawful.
- The worker must be at least 21 years old for domestic work in Saudi
  Arabia.
- Working conditions under the updated regulation: daily working hours are
  capped (10 hours), with a paid weekly rest day, paid annual leave, and
  end-of-service entitlements.
- Wages are paid through the approved digital channels linked to Musaned.
- Health insurance is mandatory for new domestic-worker contracts.
- Passport confiscation is prohibited.
- Ethiopia and Saudi Arabia have a bilateral labour agreement covering
  domestic workers; recruitment must follow both countries' rules.

Exact costs, salary floors, and Musaned process details change — ask us in
chat and our team will confirm the current figures for your case.

## DOC: Kuwait, Qatar, Bahrain, Oman — domestic worker rules in brief

Current to early 2026 — our team re-checks these regularly. Ask us in chat
for the latest details for your specific country.

Kuwait (Law No. 68 of 2015 on Domestic Workers):
- Maximum 12 working hours per day with rest; one paid weekly day off;
  30 days paid annual leave; end-of-service benefit of one month's wage per
  year of service. The employer provides food, housing, and medical care.
  A ministerial minimum wage applies to domestic workers [confirm current
  amount]. Hiring only through licensed recruitment offices; passport
  confiscation is banned.

Qatar (Law No. 15 of 2017 on Domestic Workers):
- Maximum 10 working hours per day; one paid weekly rest day (24
  consecutive hours); three weeks of paid annual leave; end-of-service
  benefit of at least three weeks' wage per year. Qatar's minimum wage law
  (1,000 QAR/month basic, plus food and housing allowances if not provided
  in kind) applies to domestic workers.

Bahrain:
- Recruitment and work permits run through the LMRA. Domestic workers are
  covered by parts of the labour law (contracts, wage protection); a
  standard tripartite contract between employer, worker, and agency is
  required.

Oman:
- Domestic workers are hired through agencies licensed by the Ministry of
  Labour with an approved employment contract; the employer sponsors the
  visa and provides food, accommodation, and medical care. A weekly rest
  day and paid leave terms are set in the contract.

## DOC: Ethiopia — legal requirements for working abroad as a domestic worker

Ethiopian law (Overseas Employment Proclamation No. 923/2016, as amended by
Proclamation No. 1246/2021) protects Ethiopian citizens taking domestic
work abroad. What every candidate must know (current to early 2026):

- Overseas placement is legal only through agencies licensed by the
  Ethiopian government, or government-to-government programs. Ethiopia must
  have a labour agreement with the destination country.
- Minimum age is 18 (destination rules may be stricter — Saudi Arabia
  requires 21).
- An eighth-grade education is the minimum schooling requirement set by the
  proclamation.
- Before departure every worker must complete: an occupational competence
  certificate (COC) for domestic work, a medical fitness examination,
  pre-departure orientation training, and mandatory insurance coverage.
- The employment contract must be registered with the Ethiopian authorities
  before travel, and the worker keeps her own passport at all times.
- Workers must never pay illegal broker ("delala") fees. Report anyone
  demanding money for a job to the authorities and to us.

We guide every candidate through these steps as part of the placement
process — ask us in chat at any point.

## DOC: Interviews — how video interviews work

When a sponsor wants to meet a candidate, we arrange a video interview:

- The sponsor picks a convenient time in chat ("tomorrow 2pm", "today
  4pm"). We create the booking and send the video call link right here in
  WhatsApp.
- Reminders are sent automatically 1 hour and 10 minutes before the call.
- The candidate is notified through her Ethiopian Maids app with the same
  link.
- The call is a normal browser video call — no app installation is needed;
  tap the link on your phone at the scheduled time.
- Interviews are free and there is no limit — sponsors can interview more
  than one candidate before deciding.
- If the time no longer suits you, tell us in chat and we will reschedule.

Practical tips for sponsors: prepare your key questions (experience with
children or elderly, cooking, languages), and check the candidate's
expected salary and start date shown on her card.
